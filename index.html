❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is ComputePH really free to use?

Yes. All calculators and the QR code generator are 100% free, with no signup required. Optional premium tools may be added in the future.

Does ComputePH store my financial data?

No data is sent to any server. Calculations run entirely in your browser, and history is saved only in your device's local storage.

Can I use ComputePH offline?

Yes. ComputePH is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) that works offline after your first visit.
